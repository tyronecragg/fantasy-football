Odds provenance, 2026-27 season (moved from SYNTHETIC_NOTE.txt on 2026-08-21; this file is
documentation only - the --gw archive guard does NOT read it).

REAL, scraped from Betway (tools/betway.py):
  - 1X2 (win/draw/win), team goals, clean sheets
  - PLAYER goalscorer, 2+ goals, assists, 2+ assists

REAL, collected by hand from Ladbrokes (tools/ladbrokes_cards.py):
  - Yellow CARDS (sportsbet_booking_odds.csv): real "to be booked" odds for every listed
    player. Every other roster player in the two teams of a COMPLETE match is filled with
    that match's max (longest) captured price - a low, conservative booking chance, not a
    true per-player quote. This game-max fill is a STANDING feature of the weekly workflow.

DERIVED from real data (no market exists on Betway):
  - Goalkeeper saves: built from the real shots-on-target ladders (betway.py derive_saves).
    The SoT->goals CONVERSION (0.30) was validated 2026-08-20 against real Ladbrokes match
    shots-on-target lines and Bet365 save ladders (both independently ~0.30).

SYNTHETIC placeholders (harmless to the archive - only F1 columns are archived):
  - GW2 team markets (*_f2 clean sheet / team goals) until bookmakers price the next GW.

ARCHIVE POLICY (decided 2026-08-21): plain `python -m fpl_pipeline.run --gw N` archives BOTH
match odds and player history every week. The accepted trade-off: unlisted fringe players'
yellow-card factors seed from the conservative game-max fill price; the trailing medians
smooth this over subsequent weeks, and the alternative was no card history at all.
